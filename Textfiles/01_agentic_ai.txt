Agentic AI is an AI system that can understand a goal, create a plan, use available tools, 
take actions, observe the results, and improve its approach until the goal is achieved with minimal human intervention.

Key characteristics
Summary Table


Characteristic	        Meaning

Goal-Oriented	        Works to achieve a specific goal
Autonomous	            Performs tasks with minimal human help
Planning	            Breaks tasks into smaller steps
Decision-Making	        Chooses the best action
Tool Usage	            Uses APIs, browsers, databases, etc.
Reasoning	            Thinks logically to solve problems
Learning	            Improves using feedback
Adaptability	        Adjusts to changing situations
Multi-Step Execution	Handles complex tasks step by step
Continuous Monitoring	Tracks progress and retries if needed
